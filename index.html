About Me 个人简介

I am an incoming graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) pursuing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence Engineering-BioMedical Engineering (Fall 2026), and a final-year undergraduate at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU) majoring in Information and Computing Science.

My research passion lies in developing reliable AI systems for healthcare, human-computer interaction, and affective computing. I specialize in designing multimodal neural networks, memory-augmented transformers, and physiological cognitive synchrony models to capture subtle behavioral and emotional cues. I am proficient in Python (PyTorch, TensorFlow) and Java (Spring Boot), with fluent English (TOEFL 107) and native Chinese language skills.

Beyond the lab and software engineering, I am an accomplished pianist (Level 10 certification) and street dancer (Level 4), believing that the discipline and creativity of art profoundly benefit scientific innovation and complex system design.

Selected Publications & Research 代表性论文与研究

First Author | IEEE TAFFC (Published)
Phy-FusionNet: A Memory-Augmented Transformer for Multimodal Emotion Recognition
Wu, T., Purwanto, E., Huang, Y. & Su, Y. (2025) DOI: 10.1109/TAFFC.2025.3609046
Addressed the limitation of capturing subtle emotional cues by designing a memory stream module to preserve long-term context. Incorporated a Fourier-based periodicity modeling module for physiological rhythms and a hybrid attention head. Achieved 13.75% improvement over standard transformers and 4.86% absolute accuracy gains on WESAD, CL-Drive, and PPB-Emo datasets.
针对捕捉细微情感线索的难题，创新性设计了 memory stream（记忆流）模块以保留长时上下文。结合傅里叶周期性建模处理生理节律及混合注意力机制，该模型在 WESAD、CL-Drive 和 PPB-Emo 数据集上较标准 Transformer 提升了 13.75% 的性能，绝对准确率提升 4.86%。
Memory Stream Fourier Analysis Transformer
First Author | ACMMM 2026 (Submitted)
True Consensus from Subtleties - Detecting Cognitive Synchrony in Two-person Interactions 从细微处见真共识 - 双人交互中的认知同步检测
Wu, T., Purwanto, E. & Liang, H. (2026)
Constructed a distinct Physio-Consensus dataset using the Hidden Profile paradigm and EmotiBit wearable sensors (EDA/IMU) to distinguish "True Consensus" from surface-level "False Consensus" and groupthink. Proposed a Physio-Transformer framework for interpersonal feature fusion, achieving an F1-score of 0.82 and confirming structural dyadic coupling via CRQA (R=0.747).
基于隐藏剖面范式（Hidden Profile）和 EmotiBit 可穿戴传感器（EDA/IMU）构建了生理共识数据集，用于区分群体决策中的“真共识”与表面“假共识”。提出了用于人际特征融合的 Physio-Transformer 框架，实现了 0.82 的 F1-score，并通过交叉复现定量分析（CRQA）验证了二元耦合的全局同步率（R=0.747）。

ML Framework for CES Columns Load Capacity Prediction
Jun. 2025 | XGBoost, Optuna, SHAP, TOPSIS
Designed and evaluated an interpretable ML framework for predicting the load-carrying capacity of Concrete-Encased Steel (CES) columns using a hybrid dataset (96 experimental + 300 KDE-generated samples). Optimized 7 regression models using Optuna and composite scoring (AHP/TOPSIS), identifying XGBoost as optimal. Utilized SHAP values and partial dependence plots to ensure alignment with structural engineering principles. 设计并评估了一个可解释的机器学习框架，基于混合数据集（96组实验数据 + 300组KDE生成样本）预测全包钢混凝土（CES）柱的极限承载力。使用 Optuna 优化了7种回归模型，并通过 AHP/TOPSIS 综合评分确定 XGBoost 为最优模型。结合 SHAP 值与局部依赖图进行归因，确保模型符合结构工程原理。
Kaggle - RSNA 2024 Lumbar Spine Degenerative Classification
